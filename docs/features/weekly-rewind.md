# Your weekly Rewind

Rewind brings the memories you have saved into a story you can revisit. It works locally,
including for someone who has only just started a journal.

The overview opens on the previous completed week. The period selector also offers **This week
so far**, which includes memories saved since the preferred start of the current week. An empty
previous week offers a direct path to this week; an empty current week offers the entry editor.
There is no minimum journal history or wait for the end of the week.

Opening this week's saved story reuses its snapshot. **Update Rewind** brings in newer memories
while keeping the saved story's identity, viewing history and attached responses. The saved
story remains available during preparation and after a failed update. Switching periods cancels
preparation owned by the previous selection. Current-week observation follows local week boundaries
and time-zone changes.

Phone and larger-screen layouts use the same period selection, saved stories and playback.
History shows one weekly cover when an earlier current-week snapshot and its completed week
both exist. Milestone stories remain distinct.
